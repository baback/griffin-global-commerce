Griffin Global Commerce is a Canadian e-commerce consultancy that helps businesses raise capital through crowdfunding, launch and manage online stores across platforms like Amazon and Shopify, and expand into international markets through strategic trade facilitation. We provide end-to-end support from strategy to fulfillment.

We work across a wide range of industries including consumer products and electronics, health and wellness, food and agriculture, technology, sustainable and eco-friendly products, and fashion and lifestyle. Our diverse experience allows us to adapt our strategies to fit your specific market.

We start with a discovery call to understand your product and goals. From there, we handle market assessment, campaign strategy, creative development, launch management, backer engagement, and post-campaign fulfillment. We manage the entire lifecycle so you can focus on your product.

We primarily work with Kickstarter and Indiegogo for crowdfunding campaigns. For e-commerce, we manage stores on Amazon, Shopify, and other major marketplaces. We'll recommend the right platform mix based on your product and target audience.

Timelines vary depending on the scope. A crowdfunding campaign typically takes 8–12 weeks from strategy to launch. E-commerce store setup can be completed in 4–6 weeks. International trade engagements are ongoing partnerships. We'll provide a clear timeline during our initial consultation.

Our fee structure depends on the services you need. We offer project-based pricing for campaigns and store launches, and retainer-based pricing for ongoing management. Contact us for a custom quote tailored to your specific needs.
