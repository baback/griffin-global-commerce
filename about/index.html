About Us

A Canadian platform focused on growth, opportunity, and long-term value.

Griffin Global Commerce Ltd. is a Vancouver-based company operating at the intersection of business development, investment thinking, crowdfunding, commercial execution, and market expansion.

We work with businesses that are building toward growth, and we are equally interested in engaging with investors who are looking for access to opportunities with strong fundamentals and meaningful upside potential.

Our role is not limited to one industry or one channel. We take a broader view, looking at businesses and opportunities through the lens of commercial viability, scalability, market readiness, and long-term value creation.

Our Story

Built around opportunity and execution.

Griffin Global Commerce was built on the belief that many promising businesses never reach their full potential because they lack the right combination of structure, execution, and capital alignment.

Some need better strategic direction. Some need support entering the market. Some need access to funding. Others represent investment opportunities that require the right commercial lens and growth platform to move forward.

We created Griffin Global Commerce Ltd. to operate in that space — where business opportunity, practical execution, and capital readiness meet.

Our experience also includes hands-on exposure to real commercial activity through internal brands such as Grifglo and Celmin, and through channels including Amazon, Walmart, and Shopify. That operating experience strengthens the way we assess opportunities and support growth.

Our Mission

What we are here to do.

Our mission is to identify, support, and help develop businesses and opportunities with real growth potential by bringing together strategic thinking, practical execution, and a capital-oriented perspective.

Our Vision

Where we are headed.

Our vision is to build a trusted Canadian platform that connects businesses and investors through credible opportunities, practical support, and long-term growth thinking.

Our Principles

What guides our approach.

Clarity

Strong opportunities deserve clear thinking and honest evaluation.

Execution

Ideas only matter when they can be turned into action.

Discipline

Good growth requires structure, selectivity, and sound judgment.

Partnership

The best opportunities are built through aligned relationships.

Long-Term Value

We focus on meaningful growth rather than short-term noise.

Our Difference

Why our model is different.

Many firms operate in narrow lanes — advisory only, capital only, or operations only. Griffin Global Commerce takes a broader and more integrated approach.

We look at businesses and opportunities from multiple angles: growth potential, operational reality, market access, capital needs, and strategic fit. This allows us to engage more thoughtfully with both businesses and investors.
