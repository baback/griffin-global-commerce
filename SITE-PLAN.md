# Griffin Global Commerce — Website Plan

## Concept

Griffin Global Commerce Ltd. is positioned as a Canadian business platform focused on growth, investment, crowdfunding, and market development. The website serves as a corporate introduction — clean, professional, not overly heavy. It clearly explains what the company does, who it works with, and what opportunities/services it focuses on.

Two primary audiences: (1) Businesses / Founders, (2) Investors.

---

## Pages

| Page | Path | Description |
|------|------|-------------|
| Home | `/index.html` | Main landing — hero, who we are, three pillars, for businesses, for investors, operating experience, why Griffin, closing CTA |
| About | `/about/index.html` | Company story, mission, vision, guiding principles, differentiator, closing CTA |
| Services | `/services/index.html` | Four services (growth strategy, crowdfunding/capital, investment review, commercial execution), who we work with, closing CTA |
| For Investors | `/investors/index.html` | Investor-focused — hero, how we evaluate opportunities, closing CTA |
| Contact | `/contact/index.html` | Contact form, contact details, location |

---

## Design Direction
- Dark navy background (`#060B18`)
- Gold accents (`#D4A843` / `#F6D365`)
- DM Sans for headings, Inter for body
- Tailwind CSS via CDN
- Iconify (Solar icon set)
- Enterprise card layouts with subtle gold borders on hover
- Consistent nav + footer across all pages
- Mobile-responsive with hamburger menu

## Key Brand Notes
- Griffin Global Commerce Ltd. = main corporate identity
- Grifglo and Celmin = internal brands / operating experience only

- Tone: corporate, credible, growth-oriented
- No exaggerated language or guaranteed returns (especially on investor pages)
