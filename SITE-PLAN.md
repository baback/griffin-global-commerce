# Griffin Global Commerce — Website Redesign Plan

## Concept

Inspired by KarenCrowd (Iranian crowdfunding platform), adapted for a **Canadian e-commerce & crowdfunding consultancy**. The KarenCrowd model is: investment platform → project diversity → transparent evaluation → returns. Griffin's version: we help businesses raise capital through crowdfunding, launch e-commerce, and expand globally — acting as the **bridge between entrepreneurs and capital/markets**.

---

## Pages

### 1. Home (`/index.html`)
The main landing page. Enterprise, clean, navy+gold.

**Sections:**
- **Hero**: "Connecting Entrepreneurs to Capital & Global Markets" — position Griffin as the bridge between product creators and funding/sales channels
- **What We Do** (3 pillars):
  - **Crowdfunding Campaigns** — We help businesses raise capital through Kickstarter, Indiegogo, and other platforms
  - **E-Commerce Launch** — From Amazon to Shopify, we build and manage your online sales channels
  - **International Trade** — Supply chain, logistics, and market entry for global expansion
- **Why Griffin** (3 cards, mirrors KarenCrowd's "Why Invest" section):
  - **Diverse Projects** — We work across industries: consumer products, health & wellness, tech, food & agriculture, and more
  - **Rigorous & Transparent Process** — Every project goes through thorough evaluation by our expert team before we take it on
  - **Strong Returns** — Our track record of successful campaigns and profitable e-commerce launches speaks for itself
- **How It Works** (step-by-step process, mirrors KarenCrowd's 8-step flow, adapted):
  1. **Discovery Call** — Tell us about your product or business idea
  2. **Market Assessment** — We evaluate market fit, competition, and opportunity
  3. **Strategy & Planning** — Custom roadmap for crowdfunding, e-commerce, or both
  4. **Campaign / Store Build** — We create and launch your campaign or storefront
  5. **Marketing & Growth** — Paid media, PR, community building
  6. **Fulfillment & Logistics** — Order management, shipping, international distribution
  7. **Ongoing Optimization** — Continuous improvement and scaling
  8. **Reporting & Returns** — Transparent reporting on performance and revenue
- **CTA Banner** — "Ready to launch?" with contact button
