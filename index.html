Who We Are

A business platform built for growth.

Based in North Vancouver, Griffin Global Commerce Ltd. works with businesses, founders, and investors to identify opportunities, support expansion, and build stronger paths to growth.

Our platform brings together business development, crowdfunding, investment thinking, market expansion, and operational execution. In some cases, we support companies as a strategic and commercial partner. In others, we evaluate opportunities for direct participation, capital collaboration, or long-term growth alignment.

While we have strong experience in commerce, retail, and digital channels, our focus is broader: we are interested in businesses with real potential across multiple sectors and stages of growth.

What We Do

Three pillars of our work.

01

Business Growth & Strategic Development

We support businesses that are looking to grow, reposition, expand into new markets, improve their commercial structure, or prepare for the next stage of development.

02

Crowdfunding, Investment & Capital Opportunities

We work around capital formation and opportunity development. This may include supporting businesses that are preparing for crowdfunding or investment, as well as identifying selected opportunities where capital can be deployed strategically.

03

Market Execution & Commercial Expansion

Where relevant, we support operational execution across channels such as Amazon, Walmart, Shopify, sourcing, trade coordination, and cross-border expansion. These capabilities strengthen our practical understanding of how businesses move from concept to market.
